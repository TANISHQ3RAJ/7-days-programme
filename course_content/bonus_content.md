## 2. AI Career Roadmap

**Level 1: AI User**
- **Skills:** Basic prompting, tool familiarity.
- **Jobs:** Content creation, administration, basic marketing.
- **Value:** Saving 2-5 hours a week.

**Level 2: Prompt Engineer**
- **Skills:** System prompting, few-shot, CoT, structured outputs.
- **Jobs:** specialized content creation, data extraction, AI consultant.
- **Value:** Highly efficient workflows, replacing low-level outsourcing.

**Level 3: Context Engineer / AI Workflow Automator**
- **Skills:** RAG basics, agentic workflows, API integrations (no-code/low-code).
- **Jobs:** AI Operations Manager, Automation Specialist.
- **Value:** Automating entire business departments (e.g., customer support routing).

**Level 4: AI Systems Architect / Builder**
- **Skills:** Python, LangChain, fine-tuning models, multi-agent frameworks.
- **Jobs:** Machine Learning Engineer, AI Product Developer.
- **Value:** Creating proprietary AI products and systems.
