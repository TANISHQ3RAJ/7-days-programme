# Student Materials & Worksheets

## 1. The Ultimate Prompt Formula Cheat Sheet

Use the **CREATE** framework for structured prompting:
- **C** - Context (Who are you and what is the background?)
- **R** - Request (What exactly do you want?)
- **E** - Explanation (Why do you need this? What is the goal?)
- **A** - Action (What specific steps should the AI take?)
- **T** - Tone (How should it sound? Professional, casual, Gen-Z?)
- **E** - Extras (Format requirements, constraints, negative prompts)

**Example Prompt Template:**
> "Act as a [Role/Expertise] with experience in [Specific Field]. I need you to [Specific Task]. The goal of this is to [Desired Outcome]. Please follow these steps: [Step 1, Step 2]. Ensure the tone is [Tone]. Format the output as [Table/Markdown/Bullet points] and DO NOT include [Constraints]."

## 2. Context Engineering Worksheets

**System Prompt Builder Template:**
When building an AI Agent or using custom instructions, define the following:

1. **System Identity:** "You are an AI Assistant designed to..."
2. **Knowledge Boundaries:** "You only know about X. If asked about Y, say you don't know."
3. **Response Guidelines:** "Always respond in 3 sentences or less."
4. **Tool Access:** "You have access to a web search tool. Use it when..."

## 3. Project Submission Format & Rubric

**Project Title:** 
**Creator Name:**
**Tools Used:** (e.g., ChatGPT, n8n, Claude)

**1. Problem Statement:** What problem does this AI tool solve?
**2. The Solution:** How does your project solve it?
**3. Architecture/Workflow:** (Describe the flow of data or the sequence of prompts)
**4. Demonstration:** (Link to video demo or screenshot)

### Assessment Rubric (Out of 50 Points)
- **Innovation & Creativity (10 pts):** Is the idea unique or uniquely applied?
- **Prompt/Context Quality (15 pts):** Are the prompts well-structured and utilizing advanced techniques (few-shot, CoT)?
- **Functionality (15 pts):** Does the prototype work as intended using free tools?
- **Presentation (10 pts):** Is the submission clear, well-documented, and professional?

## 4. Daily Challenges

- **Day 1 Challenge:** Get an AI to admit it made a mistake and correct itself.
- **Day 2 Challenge:** Write a prompt that outputs a perfect 7-day workout plan formatted in a Markdown table.
- **Day 3 Challenge:** Upload your own resume to NotebookLM/Claude and ask it to roast your resume like a harsh recruiter.
- **Day 4 Challenge:** Generate a 5-slide presentation on quantum physics using Gamma.app in under 2 minutes.
