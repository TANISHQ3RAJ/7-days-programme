# Course Brand Identity

## 1. Course Title
**Primary Title:** Context Engineering & AI Systems Lab
**Alternative Titles:** 
- GenAI Mastery Bootcamp
- Future Intelligence Lab
- AI Builders Program

## 2. Tagline
"Architecting the Future of Human-AI Collaboration."

## 3. Brand Identity
The brand identity is designed to feel like a premium, industry-level certification program from top AI institutes.
- **Aesthetic:** Dark futuristic AI aesthetic.
- **Vibe:** Cutting-edge, authoritative, dynamic, and immersive.
- **Key Visual Elements:** Glowing gradients, holographic UI elements, clean abstract geometry.

## 4. Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** `#0B0C10` (Deep Space Black)
- **Secondary Background:** `#1F2833` (Graphite)
- **Accent 1 (Neon Cyan):** `#66FCF1` (Holographic Blue - Used for primary buttons & highlights)
- **Accent 2 (Muted Teal):** `#45A29E` (Used for secondary elements & borders)
- **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (White)
- **Text Secondary:** `#C5C6C7` (Light Silver)
- **Gradient Style:** Linear gradients blending `#66FCF1` to `#8A2BE2` (Purple) for an ultra-modern glowing effect.

## 5. Font Pairing
- **Primary / Headings:** `Space Grotesk` or `Orbitron` (Futuristic, geometric, wide tracking)
- **Secondary / Body Text:** `Inter` or `Roboto Mono` (Clean, highly readable, tech-forward)
